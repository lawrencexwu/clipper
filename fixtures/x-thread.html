1/ Quick thread on widget economics. The conventional story misses the most interesting part: pricing power has flipped between OEMs and component suppliers three times in the last decade.
May 10, 2026
@jane
2/ Round one: 2017-2019. OEMs commoditized everything below the case. Suppliers got squeezed. Margins compressed to single digits across the board.
@jane
3/ Round two: 2020-2022. Supply chain shocks. Suddenly the obscure tier-3 supplier of a niche widget had de facto pricing power over a multi-billion-dollar product line. Margins inverted.
@jane
4/ Round three: 2023-2025. OEMs aggressively vertically integrated the bottleneck parts. New leverage, new contracts, suppliers back on their heels.
@jane
5/ The lesson: in any complex hardware stack, leverage isn't permanent. Whoever currently has it should assume they will lose it within 24-36 months, and plan capital allocation accordingly.
